ABOUT THE BRAND

SHIV MENS / NAGPUR

OUR PERSPECTIVE

AUTHENTIC STREETWEAR & TIMELESS MEN'S APPAREL.

Based at Sai Vasanshah Square in Jaripatka, SHIV MENS was established to offer men in Nagpur a curated retail alternative — focused on tactile textiles, modern fits, and everyday confidence.

Rather than chasing fleeting fast-fashion gimmicks, we focus on genuine staples: comfort-stretch jeans that hold their fit through months of daily wear, breathable linen-look co-ord sets designed for Indian climates, and tailored shirts with sharp proportions.

Customers can visit our physical store to feel fabrics and try cuts in person, or connect with our store staff directly on WhatsApp for real-time sizing and fit recommendations.
